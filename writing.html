Writing

Essays, criticism, and books

Most of what I write now appears in The Hedgehog Review, where I’m a contributing editor — essays on technology and society, and on European intellectual history, written for a general audience. Alongside that is peer-reviewed work on AI and human flourishing, and the books that make up the longer scholarly record underneath it all.
